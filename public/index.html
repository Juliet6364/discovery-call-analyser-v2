How it works

  1. Add the conversation

    Paste a transcript or chat, upload a file or recording, or record a role-play here. Audio is transcribed on your device.

  2. Score the qualification

    Each element of your framework is checked and backed by a quote from the call, or flagged as not covered.

  3. Act on it

    See what to say better next time, line by line. Get a deal verdict, the questions for your next call, a follow-up email, CRM notes and a PDF report.
